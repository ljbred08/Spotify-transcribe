Improve sync

Runs a transcription-only model to get measured word timestamps, then re-aligns this transcript's words to them. Your lyrics' text and line breaks are kept — only timings change. Missing words are interpolated, misheard words take their neighbour's timing, invented reference words are dropped. The result is saved as a new version.
